Starting grids only — services, transfer zones and party walls usually drive the final layout.

  • Basements. Temporary works and underpinning sequence usually govern feasibility long before the permanent design — flag early.
  • Waterproofing. Set a BS 8102 strategy (Type A / B / C) at concept, not on site. It affects wall build-ups and finished levels.
  • Party walls. Excavation and underpinning near neighbours trigger notices under the Party Wall etc. Act 1996 — allow programme for it.
  • Adjoining structures. Movement monitoring and a condition survey are often needed where you build up to the boundary.
  • Listed & retained façades. Consent and temporary support shape what's structurally possible — bring the engineer in early.
